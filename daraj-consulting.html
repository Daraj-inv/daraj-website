Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

Do you help with processes, KPIs and OKRs?

Yes. Our Process Excellence and Performance System projects design SOPs, SLAs, KPIs and OKRs. If you want us to run them every month, our Performance department does that.

Do you work in my industry?

We work with businesses in every industry. For each project we bring in specialists with direct experience of your type of business. If we can’t help, we tell you honestly.

Is my business too small, or too big?

No. From a single shop to a large group: the method is the same, the team and scope change with your size.

How much does it cost?

The Business X-Ray is free. After it, you receive a written proposal with a fixed fee for a clear scope. No open-ended hourly bills.

Do I have to use your implementation team?

No. Consulting is complete on its own: you get the solution, the roadmap and a handover workshop. Implementation is a separate, optional service.

How do you measure results?

We agree the measures before we start (hours, cost, revenue, cash, quality), record the baseline, and follow up at 30, 60 and 90 days.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality agreement before you share any data.
