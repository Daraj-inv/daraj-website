Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

One monthly fee per department, based on your company size. You receive a written proposal after a free call.

Is this the same as consulting?

No. Consulting designs the solution and your team carries it out. Departments runs the function for you every month.

Can I start with one department?

Yes. Many clients start with one and add more as they see results.

Who does licensed work like tax filing or legal?

Licensed partners do it; we coordinate and report.

Is my data safe?

Yes. NDAs and data-handling terms are part of every contract.
