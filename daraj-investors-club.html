Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

Can you find opportunities in a specific country?

Yes. Tell us the country, sector and ticket size. We research the market, search for opportunities and check them before you see them.

Will I meet other investors?

Yes. Members meet other verified investors at the Daraj Majlis and through introductions, to share deals and co-invest.

Is membership really free?

Yes. Joining and receiving reviewed opportunities is free.

Does Daraj give investment advice?

No. We review and present opportunities and help you check them. The decision is always yours.

Does my money go through Daraj?

Never. You invest directly in the company, through a bank or escrow.

What is Investor Care?

After you invest through Daraj, we help you follow the business: quarterly updates, early warnings and support with later decisions.
